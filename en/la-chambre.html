The Chamber

A professional body affiliated with UTICA, the Chamber brings Tunisia's private clinics together around a shared ambition: quality care that is accessible and trustworthy.

  • Mission
  • Executive board
  • Statutes
  • History

Mission

Represent the profession, defend its interests and contribute, alongside public authorities, to the quality of the Tunisian health system.

Represent

Speak for private clinics before the Ministry of Health, CNAM and other institutions.

Defend

Negotiate agreements and help shape the sector's regulatory framework.

Support

Inform and advise members on regulation, quality, human resources and investment.

Promote

Showcase Tunisian healthcare in Tunisia and abroad.

Statutes

The Chamber is governed by its statutes and internal rules, within the framework of UTICA's statutes. Any licensed private healthcare facility operating in Tunisia may join.
