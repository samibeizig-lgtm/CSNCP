Medical travel · Tunisia

Get treatment in Tunisia

Modern clinics, recognised doctors and a warm welcome, a few hours' flight from Europe, North Africa and sub-Saharan Africa.

Why choose Tunisia

Recognised expertise

Specialists often trained in Tunisia and abroad, and teams experienced in caring for international patients.

Proximity

Two to three hours' flight from most European capitals, with regular connections to Africa and the Middle East.

Controlled costs

Competitive prices, announced in advance through a detailed quote, for care that meets international standards.

Modern equipment

Up-to-date facilities: imaging, operating theatres, interventional cardiology, haemodialysis.

Several languages

Care in Arabic, French and English, and often Italian or German.

A setting for recovery

Mild climate, a tradition of thalassotherapy and a wide range of hotels for a peaceful recovery.

Most requested treatments

  • Cosmetic and reconstructive surgery
  • Orthopaedics and spine surgery
  • Ophthalmology and refractive surgery
  • Cardiology and cardiovascular surgery
  • Assisted reproduction
  • Haemodialysis during your stay
  • Comprehensive health check-ups

How it works

  1. First contactSend your medical file to the clinic chosen from the directory.
  2. Medical opinion and quoteThe doctor reviews your file; the clinic sends you a detailed quote.
  3. Travel and stayWelcome, hospital stay and follow-up by the medical team.
  4. Return and follow-upA medical report is handed over at discharge for your own doctor.

Our advice before you travel

  • Contact a member clinic directly and ask for a detailed written quote.
  • Check whether your insurer or health fund may cover the treatment.
  • Ask for the name and specialty of the doctor who will treat you.
  • Plan a long enough stay for post-operative follow-up before flying home.
